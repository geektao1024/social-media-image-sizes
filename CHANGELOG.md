# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First release, exported from the size catalog behind [cropden.com](https://cropden.com).

- 14 platforms, 55 sizes in 20 groups, with safe zones, upload limits and minimum sizes.
- Specs for 6 passport and visa photos: pixel size, print size, head and eye-line ranges.
- JSON in `data/social-media-image-sizes.json`, also served at https://cropden.com/data/social-media-image-sizes.json.
- Agent skill `skills/social-media-image-sizes`: crops images to a size on your machine and draws the covered areas on a preview.
- MCP server at https://cropden.com/mcp: free size lookups, plus AI extend, upscale and background removal paid with CropDen credits.

Next (0.2.0): a source link for every group.
